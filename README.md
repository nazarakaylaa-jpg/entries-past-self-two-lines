A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) entry for **Two Lines to Your Past Self**.

`main.ts` writes two lines to `/output/entry.txt`: a warning to the past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why that moment mattered and what changed because of it.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes `/output/entry.txt` (208 characters, exactly two lines, within the 1–280 character gate).
